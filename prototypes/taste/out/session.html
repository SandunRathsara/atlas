Spec

Goal

Webhook deliveries that fail are dropped after one attempt. Retry them with exponential backoff so customers stop missing events.

Acceptance

  • Failed deliveries retry at 1s, 2s, 4s … capped at 5 minutes
  • Jitter prevents retry storms
  • Gives up after 24 hours and marks the delivery failed
  • Tests cover backoff and give-up
